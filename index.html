Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Call (519) 442-0132 or send us a message above.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! Louvre Dental Centre warmly welcomes new patients of all ages, from children to seniors. Request an appointment using the form above or call (519) 442-0132.

Do you offer same-day emergency dental care?

We reserve time each day for dental emergencies such as toothaches, broken or knocked-out teeth, lost fillings and swelling. Call us as early as possible and we'll do our best to see you the same day.

Do you accept CDCP and dental insurance?

Yes. We accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) and offer direct billing to most major insurance providers, so you only pay any remaining balance after coverage.

Where are you located and is there parking?

We're at 1070 Rest Acres Rd, Unit G7, Paris, ON N3L 0K6, with free on-site parking and accessible, ground-floor access.

What are your opening hours?

We're open Monday to Friday 10:00 AM–7:00 PM and Saturday 10:00 AM–3:00 PM. Evening appointments are available for busy schedules.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely. We provide gentle kids dentistry, and our dentists are experienced with young patients to make every visit a positive one.
